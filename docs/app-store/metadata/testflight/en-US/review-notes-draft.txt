Night Owl UB is a free Ulaanbaatar social/place-discovery app, primarily in Mongolian, for an adult community. This beta connects to the staging backend. It contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, payments or ticket sales.

Guest Explore opens the map and venue details; location permission is optional. Use the persistent, non-admin review account supplied in the Sign-In Information fields and sign in with email. Sign in with Apple is also enabled for new accounts; Google sign-in is not enabled in this beta. On first login, normal onboarding opens: confirm or enter a username (3–20 letters, digits, underscores or periods), personally select the 18+ and community-rules checkbox, then save. Display name, bio, interests and avatar are optional; no date of birth is requested. Age and community-rules acceptance have not been pre-filled on the reviewer's behalf.

Create opens the post/story/short-video flows. Posts/Reels, stories, comments, notes, venue reviews, public collections and profile details are published immediately; every user must first accept the 18+ community rules during onboarding. Safeguards: (1) Filtering — the server checks new and edited text (captions, comments, notes, reviews, collection titles, profile names, bios and interests, free-text place names and story music titles) against a maintained list of prohibited terms (slurs, child-sexual-abuse terms, hard-drug terms) and holds matches, and text containing web links or phone numbers (usernames excepted), for moderator review before anyone else can see it. An edit that adds new media to a published post or story, and any edit to content a moderator rejected, also waits for review. Images and video are not analysed automatically. (2) Reporting — posts/Reels, stories, comments, notes, reviews, collections, events, venues, profiles and received messages have a Report action. A reported post/Reel, story, comment, note, review or collection disappears for the reporting account after refresh, and is hidden from everyone once 3 different established accounts (public profile, account at least 24 hours old) have reported it; a profile is hidden once 5 have. Event, venue and message reports go to the moderator queue without automatic hiding. (3) Response — the developer moderates reports and held content and aims to act within 24 hours, removing objectionable content and banning offending accounts. (4) Blocking — user options include Block, which removes that user's posts from the blocker's feed and stops new direct interaction. Venue and event listings are approved before they appear. Approved sample posts from another account are already in the feed, so you can try reporting and blocking on them. Build 3 may still show an older "sent for review" message after posting; a later build corrects this text.

You may test account deletion (Profile > Settings > Бүртгэл устгах) with the supplied account. Deletion is permanent; for Sign in with Apple accounts it also revokes the Apple token.
