Night Owl UB is a free social discovery app for Ulaanbaatar. The main interface is Mongolian.

Access
Guest Explore supports map and venue browsing without device location. Publishing, follows, saved places and messaging require sign-in. Enter a non-admin review account separately in Sign-in Information, with completed setup and representative licensed, safe, approved content. New accounts may show profile setup and an explicit 18+ community-rules acknowledgment. Do not put credentials in these notes.

Suggested walkthrough
1. Open Explore, search/filter venues, change zoom and select a place for details and source links. Listings do not certify current opening or operation.
2. Use Create for a photo/video post or story, or open the short-video composer for one Reel. A canonical venue from the app's list can be added, changed or removed before submission; tags are optional. A retry preserves the submitted ID, media, caption and place.
3. Public content is published immediately with safeguards. A server filter holds new or edited text containing prohibited terms, web links or phone numbers (usernames excepted) for moderator review; edits that add media to a published post or story, and edits of rejected content, also wait. A reported post/Reel, story, comment, note, review or collection disappears for the reporter after refresh and is hidden from everyone after 3 distinct established reporters (a profile after 5) until a moderator decides; event, venue and message reports go to the moderator queue. The developer aims to act on reports within 24 hours and can remove content or ban accounts; users can block others. Venue and event listings still wait for approval. Authors see a pending or rejected notice only on held content. This is a keyword filter plus human review, not AI image analysis.
4. Map emotions use approved content readable by the viewer: 0, 1-4, 5-14 and 15+ tagged items. Stories count for the current Ulaanbaatar day while active; posts/Reels count for 168 hours from original publication, not approval/edit/retry. Posts and tags remain afterward. Activity is not a crowd or opening-status estimate.
5. Profile offers personal photos/owl avatars, appearance and privacy/contact settings. User options include blocking. Report post/Reel via its menu; story, comment, venue/review, event, note and collection have exact-content report actions. Long-press a received DM/group message, or use its accessible report action, to report only that selected message. Moderators do not receive general access to private inboxes or thread history.

Account deletion
Profile > Settings > account deletion is also available during unfinished setup. Apple-linked accounts require fresh native or web Apple authorization without changing the app login. Use only the separate disposable account for destructive review. Completion is confirmed by the server; interrupted cleanup may already have removed data. A saved secure receipt allows explicit status recovery from the signed-out landing screen after restart/Auth deletion. It cannot initiate a new erasure or sign out another account. This path must pass hosted/device acceptance before submission.

Payment scope
No subscriptions, in-app purchases, ticket sales or payment processing. Any venue/event admission price is organizer information; an RSVP is not a ticket. Attributed OpenStreetMap listings and available published content may leave sections empty. No calls, streaming, delivered remote push, live crowd or guaranteed opening claims.
