Find your next place in Ulaanbaatar with Night Owl UB. Explore local cafes, restaurants, bars and clubs, then share the moments that make a night your own.

EXPLORE THE CITY
Browse a street map, search places, filter venue categories and save favorites. Open directions and view venue details with source links. Discover venue-linked posts, short videos and featured events when available.

SHARE YOUR NIGHT
Publish photos, videos and stories. Add a place from Night Owl's venue list when you want to connect a moment to that location. Follow creators and keep up with the people you follow.

A MAP WITH PERSONALITY
Cute 3D owls and flames reflect venue-tagged content: today's active stories in Ulaanbaatar time plus posts from the last seven days. Sleeping, waking, lively and fiery characters show content activity, not live crowd levels or whether a business is open. Posts and their place tags remain after the seven-day activity window ends.

MAKE IT YOURS
Create a profile with your own photo or an owl avatar. Connect through follows and direct messages, manage private-account and contact preferences, and report content or block an account from the app. Choose Day, Night or System appearance.

Night Owl UB is free to use, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. The app does not sell tickets or process payments. Venue and event admission prices, when shown, come from their organizers; they do not mean every venue or event is free. An RSVP records interest or attendance and is not a ticket.

The main interface is in Mongolian. Night Owl's community is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Place information and event details can change, so confirm hours, availability and admission directly with the venue or organizer before visiting.
