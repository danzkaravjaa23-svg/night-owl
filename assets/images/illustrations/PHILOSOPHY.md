# Night Owl — Illustration System: "Neon Cartography"

A visual philosophy for the Night Owl illustration set — the quiet conceptual DNA woven through
every onboarding hero, permission glyph, and empty state.

## The movement
**Neon Cartography.** The night city mapped not as streets but as *signals* — glowing vectors,
orbiting nodes, constellations of presence. Each illustration is an instrument panel for the
after-dark: spare line-work that behaves like a readout from a discipline that studies nightlife
itself. Nothing is decorative; every line is a measurement.

## Form & space
Single-stroke geometry on a void. One central motif, breathing in generous negative space, framed
by a faint orbital ring that implies the glass medallion it lives in. Forms are built from precise
arcs, hexagons, and thin circuit traces — never filled illustration, always *drawn light*. The eye
should rest, then find the small satellite details that reward a second look.

## Color & material
Pure void black as the ground; light is the only material. Electric cyan (#22E7FF) carries the
primary structure; a magenta→purple→pink gradient (#7C3AED→#C026D3→#FF2D8E) marks the living,
human element; lime (#B4FF2E) flags what is *live* and amber (#FFBB20) what is *waiting*. Colour is
information, never fill. Glow is intentionally NOT baked into the vector — it is conferred by the
surrounding surface, so each mark stays crisp and surgical.

## Scale, rhythm & hierarchy
A strict 240-unit canvas. The hero shape occupies the central third; satellite dots and tick-marks
orbit at consistent radii, creating a metronome of small repeated elements — the patient
accumulation of marks that signals master craft. One bright focal node anchors each piece.

## Craft
Each illustration must read as the product of painstaking attention — meticulously balanced, every
arc tuned, the work of someone at the top of their field who labored over a single curve until it
sang. No clutter, no cartoon. Restraint is the proof of expertise.

## The set
A cohesive family — onboarding (nightlife, live, map), permission (location, notify), and empty
states (feed, profile, messages, notifications, creator) — all speaking one visual language so the
app feels authored, not assembled.
